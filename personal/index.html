I'm Davy. I ship full-stack products — the kind where you own the whole path from a database schema to the last pixel of a hover state, and the CI job that puts it all in front of real users.

Most of my day is ordinary and satisfying: modelling data, designing APIs that don't surprise anyone, making interfaces that feel effortless, and quietly deleting more code than I add. I like systems that are boring in production and delightful to use.

The rest of my life happens in two places. With a novel in hand — the longer, the denser, the better. Or in front of a film, rewinding a scene to work out how it was built.

Those two halves eventually refused to stay separate, which is how the dream below started taking up all my side projects.

The Dream

One click, novel → movie

I believe every classic novel and every great biography already contains a film. The script is in there, the shots are in there, the performances are in there — waiting to be extracted. My dream is a pipeline that does the extraction, so anyone can watch the books they love instead of only reading them.

01 Ingest

Drop in a book

Plain text, EPUB, or a scanned edition. Any language, any length — a 900-page biography is just a longer input, not a different problem.

02 Understand

Build a story graph

Characters, relationships, locations, timelines, and the emotional curve of the whole book — extracted once and reused by every later stage.

03 Adapt

Cut it into a screenplay

Beats become scenes, scenes become industry-format script pages with dialogue, action, and a runtime budget. This is where the hard editorial judgement lives.

04 Design

Direct it before shooting

Shot lists, storyboards, character sheets, wardrobe and location bibles, casting voices, and a score direction — a production plan, not a prompt.

05 Generate

Render the shots

Image and video models generate each shot from the boards, keeping faces, wardrobe, and light consistent across scenes. Text-to-speech performs the dialogue.

06 Assemble

Deliver the film

Automated edit, sound mix, subtitle timing, and colour pass. Out comes a finished cut with a title card — not a slideshow, not a summary. A film.

I believe my dream can come true very soon.

Roadmap

Where the dream stands

A firm belief is not a plan. So here is the plan — roughly in the order the problems actually have to fall.

Now

In progress
  • Reading and parsing full-length novels without losing the thread
  • Chapter-level segmentation and summarisation that respects structure
  • Character and relationship extraction into a queryable story graph

Next

Hard part
  • Beat-to-scene conversion with real editorial rules and runtime targets
  • Character consistency across every shot — the make-or-break problem
  • Voice casting and dialogue performance that doesn't sound generated

Later

The goal
  • Automated editing, scoring, and a full sound mix
  • Region-aware, culturally accurate adaptation choices
  • One click: title in, film out.
